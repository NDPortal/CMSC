### 3. Accounts, Verification & Offline Mode Security

* **Offline Authentication Mode**: Our dedicated game servers operate in "offline" (unauthenticated) mode and do not validate accounts through Mojang’s centralized authentication infrastructure.
* **MC:CS Verification System & Implementation Details**: To manage player connections without Mojang authentication, the Game implements an in-house gateway verification system ("MC:CS"):
  * When signing in through the custom launcher, players connect to a lightweight gatekeeper gateway that displays a temporary 5-digit verification code.
  * Entering this verification code into the launcher confirms that the client has communicated with the gateway, prompting the launcher to initialize a local session token and client UUID for matchmaking and server routing.
  * Verification codes are stored in temporary server state (`guard_codes.json` / gateway memory) alongside generation timestamps. They are designed for immediate, single-use authentication during the login sequence.
* **Inherent Limitations of Offline Mode**:
  * Because the server operates without Mojang’s centralized public-key infrastructure, usernames are not permanently cryptographically bound to an official Minecraft license or hardware identifier.
  * The MC:CS verification mechanism validates active launcher sessions, but does **not** provide the multi-factor or cryptographic identity guarantees of official Mojang online mode.
  * If two clients attempt to connect using identical offline usernames, temporary gateway codes can be overwritten, and unauthorized players could attempt to connect under another player's chosen username if launcher credentials or session tokens are exposed.
* **Player Security Responsibilities**:
  * You are solely responsible for securing your local device, launcher installation, and session tokens. Do not share your login details, verification codes, or launcher files with third parties.
  * We strictly prohibit attempting to spoof usernames, forge authentication packets, bypass the MC:CS gateway, intercept verification codes, or hijack sessions belonging to other active players.
  * In the event of username collisions or contested account claims, administration reserves the right to reassign or restrict offline usernames based on historical session records and launcher token logs. We cannot guarantee the recovery of accounts compromised through shared credentials or local device compromise.
